# Campsite feature complements crowd databases instead of replacing them

Park4Night, iOverlander and CamperContact each have tens to hundreds of thousands of places and active communities (network effect); a new app cannot catch up with that. We therefore build neither our own campsite search/rating nor route planning, and position `Campsite` as a private travel journal instead: users keep finding places through the specialist apps and enter them into CamperLog with an optional external link, linked to their own trip and expense data.

## Considered Options

- Build our own crowd database with ratings — rejected, hopeless against established network effects.
- Our own route planning/navigation — rejected, a fiercely contested specialist field of its own (cf. Roadtrippers), no added value over Google Maps/Park4Night.
